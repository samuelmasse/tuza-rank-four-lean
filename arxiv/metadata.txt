Title: Tuza's Conjecture for Graphs with at Most Four Edge-Disjoint Triangles

Authors: Samuel Masse

Abstract:
For a finite simple graph $G$, let $\nu(G)$ be the maximum number of pairwise
edge-disjoint triangles and let $\tau(G)$ be the minimum number of edges meeting
every triangle. We prove that $\tau(G) \leq 2\nu(G)$ whenever $\nu(G) \leq 4$. The
cases $\nu(G) \leq 3$ were proved by Parker. For $\nu(G) = 4$, a discharging
reduction based on the method of Sheng and Xiao shows that a counterexample
would have triangle support on at most twelve vertices. Marking a maximum
four-packing leaves 66 finite order-type cases. We encode these cases as SAT
instances and certify each resulting formula by an LRAT refutation. A Lean
formalization reconstructs the semantic clauses, checks the exact formulas
and all 66 refutations, and derives the stated graph theorem.
